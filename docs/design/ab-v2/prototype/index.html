Departments plan against a fixed quarterly envelope. The Q3 cap is $48,000 per department. Anything above the cap needs written approval from the VP of Operations, with a Finance sign-off. Spend below the cap is approved by the team lead and reviewed monthly.

Exceptions are logged in the quarterly review.
